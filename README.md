# Accenture Financial Performance Dashboard (FY2025)
## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing the financial and strategic performance of Accenture plc for FY2025 using Microsoft Excel.
The objective is to transform financial data from Accenture's Annual Report 2025 into meaningful insights through an executive dashboard.
The dashboard provides an overview of financial performance, revenue growth, profitability, geographical revenue distribution, and industry-wise revenue contribution.
## Business Objectives
- Analyze Accenture's financial performance for FY2025.
- Track revenue and profitability trends.
- Compare operating income and net income across financial years.
- Evaluate operating and net profit margins.
- Analyze revenue by industry and geographical region.
- Present key financial KPIs through an executive dashboard.

## Key Business Insights
- Revenue increased by 7.4% compared with FY2024.
- Operating income increased by 6.6%.
- Net income increased by 5.6%.
- Free cash flow increased by 26.2%.
- Operating margin declined by 11 basis points.
- The Americas represented the largest geographical revenue contribution.
